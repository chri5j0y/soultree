THE ROOT SYSTEM.

What holds you, feeds you, and carries you through.

A spiritual health assessment built from real chaplain work. Six roots that hold, nourish, and sustain one resilient life.

Have you ever known a person, or perhaps been a person, who gives the impression that everything is good, but you sense it would only take one small bump in the road before the house of cards comes tumbling down? It takes one to know one. I lived this way for a long time, and I have met countless others who have felt the same.

The answer is resilience: your ability to bounce back when something unexpected and unwanted occurs. The key is strengthening the different parts of your life, so when one part is injured, the healthy parts provide support for real healing and a return to wholeness.

Like a tree, your life is held up by what you cannot see. Roots anchor a tree when storms come, feed it every day, and carry it through long, dry seasons. The Root System looks at six of your roots, two for each of those jobs. When they are strong, the whole tree stands.

One Rule

Name each resource only once. If your family gives you hope, they still belong under Community, not Hope. This constraint reveals your true resources in each domain, and where you may be over-relying on one root to carry weight it was never meant to hold alone.
