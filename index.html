THE ROOT SYSTEM.

What feeds you below the surface, and what you reach toward above it.

A holistic spiritual health assessment built from real chaplain work. Six domains, three rooted below, three reaching above, forming one resilient life.

Have you ever known a person, or perhaps been a person, who gives the impression that everything is good, but you sense it would only take one small bump in the road before the house of cards comes tumbling down? It takes one to know one. I lived this way for a long time, and I have met countless others who have felt the same.

The answer is resilience: your ability to bounce back when something unexpected and unwanted occurs. The key is strengthening the different parts of your life, so when one part is injured, the healthy parts provide support for real healing and a return to wholeness.

Like a tree, your life has a system below the surface that feeds everything else, and a system above ground that reaches toward the light. Neither matters without the other.

The Roots
Unseen, foundational
Holy

What or who is set apart, or set above, anything else in your life? Your connection to the sacred, however you understand it.

Mind

When you are stressed, how do you calm down? Your tools and resources for relaxing, processing, and recovering.

Meaning

What gives your life purpose and direction? The deeper story you are living inside of.

The Branches
Visible, reaching
Hope

A source of hope is something that stands in contrast to an otherwise hopeless situation. Where do you find hope outside of your faith, your mind, your sense of meaning, your body, or your community?

Body

How does your body feel? Your physical health, movement, sleep, and your relationship with the vessel that carries you.

Community

Who shows up for you? Family, friends, neighbors, colleagues, and faith community.

One Rule

Name each resource only once. If your family gives you hope, they still belong under Community, not Hope. This constraint reveals your true resources in each domain, and where you may be over-relying on one root or branch to carry weight it was never meant to hold alone.

For each domain, move the slider to reflect your current resilience, from 1 (depleted) to 10 (thriving). Use the reflection space to name your actual resources. Remember the one rule: name each resource only once.
